# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Rough Stack a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Accept responsibility and correct mistakes.
- Focus technical disagreement on evidence and project outcomes.
- Protect private reports and personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sustained disruption, unwanted
sexual attention, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to an organization owner. Maintainers may edit,
reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
